# Deductions on which I can get tax benefit (Individual)

## Section 80C, 80CCC and 80CCD(1): what each covers
Section | Deduction | Description

80C | Deduction in respect of life insurance premia, deferred annuity, contributions to provident fund, subscription to certain equity shares or debentures, etc.
Amount paid or deposited towards life insurance premium, contribution to any Provident Fund set up by the Government, employees contribution to a recognised Provident Fund or an approved superannuation fund, contribution to deferred annuity plan, subscription to National Savings Certificates, tuition fees, payment or repayment of amounts borrowed for purposes of purchase/ construction of a residential house, and other similar payments/investments which are eligible for deduction under section 80C of the Income Tax Act.

The deduction claimed under this section cannot exceed ₹1,50,000/-

80CCC
Deduction in respect of contribution to certain Pension Fund

Amount paid towards any annuity plan of LIC or any other insurer for receiving pension from the pension fund, which is eligible for deduction u/s 80CCC.

The deduction claimed under this section cannot exceed ₹1,50,000/-

80CCD (1)
Deduction in respect of contribution to pension scheme of Central Government

Total amount paid or deposited during the year, in your account under a pension scheme notified by the Central Government which is eligible for deduction under sub-section (1) of section 80CCD.

The deduction claimed under this section cannot exceed ₹1,50,000/-

Note for 80C, 80CCC and 80CCD (1):

The aggregate deduction u/s 80C, 80CCC and 80CCD (1) cannot be more than ₹1,50,000/- or the gross total income, whichever is less.

## Section 80CCD(1B): what it covers
80CCD(1B)
Contribution to pension scheme of Central Government

Amount paid during the year in your account under a Central Government pension scheme eligible for deduction under sub-section (1B) of section 80CCD.

Note:

- The amount eligible under this sub-section is subject to a maximum limit of ₹ 50,000/-

- Further condition: No claim should have been made under sub-section (1) in respect of the same amount.
